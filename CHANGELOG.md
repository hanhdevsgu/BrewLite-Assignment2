  - Included `reviews: true` in `OrdersService.getOrderById` (`backend/src/modules/orders/orders.service.ts`) and updated `frontend/src/app/orders/[id]/page.tsx` to detect already-reviewed items and edit via `PATCH /reviews/:id` (avoiding duplicate `POST /reviews` `409 ConflictException`), plus updated cancel modal copy to mention `PENDING, PAYMENT_FAILED hoặc PAID`.

- **Activity Diagram (`c62`, `c78`) & Report Step 10 Alignment**: Clarified Step 10 (`POST /payments: nhận yêu cầu & kiểm tra Idempotency-Key`) and diamond `c78` (`Đúng chủ đơn & PENDING / PAYMENT_FAILED?`) in `docs/diagrams/activity-end-to-end.drawio`, `docs/BrewLite_Activity_Diagram_EndToEnd.drawio`, `docs/diagrams/activity-end-to-end.puml`, `docs/diagrams/activity-end-to-end.svg`, `docs/report/img/activity-diagram.png`, and `docs/report/Bao_Cao_BrewLite_v16.tex`.
